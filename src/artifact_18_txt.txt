================================================================================
                     GROW REWARDS OPERATIONAL RUNBOOK
================================================================================

1. INCIDENT: ESCROW VAULT DEPLETION
   - Symptoms: Worker logs "GrowError::InsufficientVaultLiquidity (0x1775)".
   - Action: 
     a. Transfer new SPL tokens to PDA vault:
        spl-token transfer <MINT> <AMOUNT> <VAULT_PDA_ADDRESS> --fund-recipient
     b. Verify escrow balance:
        spl-token balance --address <VAULT_PDA_ADDRESS>

2. INCIDENT: ORACLE KEY COMPROMISE
   - Symptoms: Suspicious voucher signatures detected in logs.
   - Action:
     a. Trigger emergency pause:
        ts-node scripts/admin.ts --action pause --pool "first-move-2025"
     b. Rotate oracle keypair:
        ts-node scripts/admin.ts --action update-oracle --new-oracle <NEW_PUBKEY>
     c. Unpause pool:
        ts-node scripts/admin.ts --action unpause --pool "first-move-2025"

3. PROMETHEUS / GRAFANA TELEMETRY ENDPOINTS
   - GET /healthz: Worker socket connection and block lag index.
   - GET /metrics: Disbursed rewards sum, Sybil rejection counter, and RPC latency.
